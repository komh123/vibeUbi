How to check this picture

Every box was discovered by walking the source tree, not described by a model. Each route came from app.METHOD(…) in server/index.js or from router.METHOD(…) joined to the prefix its router is mounted at — the two halves are one route, and counting them separately gets the registry wrong in both directions.

The numbers below the fold matter too. Before any rule ran, a lexer labelled every line code/comment/string and non-code lines were dropped. The first version of the secret-in-logs rule had no lexer and scored about 11% precision — nine hits, one real, the rest the words "token" and "secret" sitting in ordinary prose. VibeShip's own kernel refuses to let a rule under 90% gate anything, and refuses to let the noise be absorbed into a baseline. So the rule was rewritten, not excused.

token and tokens were then removed from the credential-name list on purpose. In this codebase they are a unit of measurement. That costs one real finding and removes nine false ones — a hole instead of a statement somebody would later sign for as real debt.
